## Refinement 1 — PP2.1-1 (China vs India OEM price gap)

**Original:** Chinese OEMs priced 20–35% below Indian equivalents. *(coalbiomassboiler.com, industrialbiomassboiler.com vendor blogs only)*

**Refined:** Chinese OEM export unit values for industrial boilers are approximately **15–35% below Indian equivalents on a USD/kg basis** (UN Comtrade/WITS HS 840211 watertube and HS 840290 other boilers, 2022–2023), with the largest gap (35%) in large-capacity watertube boilers (HS 840211, 2023: China $5.22/kg vs India $8.08/kg). HS 840290 shows narrower 13–22% global gap. Bilateral SEA destination data shows complicating product-mix effects (Chinese shipments to Indonesia bilateral are higher-spec/heavier, narrowing landed differential at the destination level), so the 20–35% range applies most cleanly at the global FOB level not destination-landed.

**New sources:**

- (R6.3) WITS/UN Comtrade HS 840290, 2022 — https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/ALL/year/2022/tradeflow/Exports/partner/WLD/product/840290

## Refinement 2 — PP2.2-3 (Palm oil mill outage cost)

**Original:** Palm oil mill boiler outage downtime cost $50,000–$183,000/day. *(beta.co.id Indonesian blog only)*

**Refined:** Daily revenue at risk from a palm oil mill boiler outage (CPO production stops entirely):
- Small mill (10–15 t FFB/hr): **$34,000–$51,000/day**
- Medium mill (30 t FFB/hr): **~$112,000/day**
- Large mill (45–60 t FFB/hr): **$168,000–$224,000/day**

Formula: Mill capacity × 20 operating hr/day × OER (19.86% national avg per MPOB 2023; up to 22% commercial target) × CPO price ($815–$895/t per Bursa Malaysia 2023–2024 benchmark, working figure $850/t). The original $50,000–$183,000/day brackets the 15–45 t FFB/hr population (the dominant installed base).

**New sources:**
- (R7.1) MPOB Overview of Malaysian Palm Oil Industry 2023 (PDF) — https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/images/overview/Overview2023.pdf
- (R7.2) MPOC CPO benchmark price 2023–2024 — https://www.mpoc.org.my/mpob-palm-oil-industry-mixed-in-first-eight-months-but-prices-output-and-yield-increase/
- (R7.3) Vortech Global Palm Oil Mill ROI Analysis (2025) — https://www.vortechglobal.com/return-on-investmentroi-of-setting-up-a-palm-oil-mill-a-comprehensive-analysis/
- (R7.4) Vilnius Tech BMEE peer-reviewed palm oil mill boiler study (2024) — https://journals.vilniustech.lt/index.php/BMEE/article/view/23679

**Confidence after:** Medium-High

## Refinement 3 — PP2.4-3 (Spare parts lead time)

**Original:** 6–12 weeks lead time for spare parts from Indian/Chinese OEM to SEA. *(coalbiomassboiler.com only)*

**Refined:** Lead times tiered by part type:
- Consumables (gaskets, sensors, in-stock items): **1–3 days to 1–2 weeks**
- Control panels: **4–8 weeks**
- Custom pressure parts (waterwall panels, superheater sections, drum components): **8–14 weeks** at fabrication
- Refractory tiles (cast, fired): **11–13 weeks ARO** (independent confirmation from York Shipley)
- Cross-border SEA freight & customs adds **2–4 weeks** above OEM-domestic shipping baseline.
- Industry-wide watertube boiler fabrication capacity stretched to **~50 weeks** for large new orders (Nationwide Boiler, post-2022 supply chain).

**New sources:**
- (R8.1) York Shipley refractory parts — https://www.aesystech.com/york-shipley-refractory.html (refractory 11–13 weeks ARO independent)
- (R8.2) Nationwide Boiler supply-chain blog — https://www.nationwideboiler.com/boiler-blog/overcoming-supply-chain-challenges-with-in-stock-boiler-program.html (industry watertube fabrication capacity stretch)
- (R8.3) Taishan Group lead-time tiered table (original vendor source, retained for tier 1–3) — https://coalbiomassboiler.com/industrial-boiler-spare-parts-delivery/

**Confidence after:** Medium-Low (cross-border SEA-specific freight delta remains assumption; SEA-specific operator interview required to validate). The original "6–12 weeks" was the right order of magnitude for pressure-part categories, but inadequately stated.
