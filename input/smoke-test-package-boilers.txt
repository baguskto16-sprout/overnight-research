## Topic

Industrial use of solid-fuel package boilers in the Indonesian textile sector — specifically boilers fired by coal or biomass at small to medium textile mills (capacity range 1–10 tonnes of steam per hour).

## Hypothesis

Operators of solid-fuel package boilers in Indonesian textile mills experience meaningful operational pain from high and volatile fuel costs (coal price swings, biomass quality variability), with limited tools to mitigate. They would pay for a service that materially reduces fuel cost or quality risk.

## Scope

In:
- Indonesian textile sector only (no other industries)
- Solid-fuel package boilers, capacity 1–10 TPH steam
- Coal and biomass fuels (rice husk, wood pellets, coal)
- Value chain: fuel suppliers → boiler OEMs → end users (textile mills)

Out:
- Other industrial sectors (food, palm oil, rubber, paper, etc.)
- Other geographies (Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, Vietnam, Philippines)
- Larger boilers (>10 TPH) and smaller (<1 TPH)
- Gas/oil-fired boilers

## Geography

In: Indonesia only (especially Java textile clusters: Bandung, Solo, Semarang)
Out: All other geographies

## Working hypotheses for pain points

Just one hypothesis for smoke test:

1. **High and volatile fuel cost** — coal prices fluctuate; biomass alternatives have quality variability that affects boiler performance

The skill should test this single hypothesis explicitly and produce 2-3 pain points related to it (with full IMI structure: description, who bears it, cost, frequency, root causes).
